WHISKandWRENFAQ

Questions, answered.

Where is the Bake Shed?

There are two honesty-box Bake Sheds: at 12 Honey Lane, Willowfield, and at Riverside Market, Willowfield. The Willowfield shed opens Saturday & Sunday, 10am–8pm — or until it sells out. Stock updates are posted on Instagram and Facebook (@whisk.and.wren).

What does Wren bake?

Custom celebration cakes, cupcakes, cake pops and treat boxes, plus the weekly shed menu: brownies, blondies, cookies, cake slices and cheesecake pots. Winning cheesecake-pot flavours, as voted by the Club: White Chocolate Raspberry, Lemon and Chocolate Orange.

How do I order a custom cake?

Use the Order page, DM @whisk.and.wren on Instagram or Facebook, or email hello@whiskandwren.com. Custom prices are confirmed by Wren on enquiry. A deposit secures your date; the remaining balance is due before collection, and date changes need as much notice as possible — Wren is one person behind the mixing bowl.

Can treats be posted?

Yes — the Parcel Box is back by popular demand: brownies, cookies, blondies and more, made with love and sent with cake.

What about Christmas?

The Christmas dessert menu: Winter Eve Box £22, Yule Logs (half £28 / full £36) and Mini Cheesecakes £5 each. Orders close 20 December; collection is 23 December.

Is Wren a proper registered baker?

Yes — Whisk & Wren is a fully registered and insured home bakery in Willowfield, food-hygiene certified, running since 2021.

Can my dog come to the shed?

Please do. Milo's Biscuit Club: buy a slice, cake or cupcake at the Willowfield shed and your dog gets a free homemade biscuit. A water bowl is always out.

Does Wren have a website?

She does now — this one. Before it, everything ran through her Instagram and Facebook, and she was happy with her socials.
